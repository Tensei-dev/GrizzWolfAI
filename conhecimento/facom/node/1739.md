# Visiting Professor in Computer Science

Concurso Docente

Application deadline: February 17, 2022 2:59pm GMT-03

**Público-alvo:** [Estudante](https://facom.ufu.br/publico-alvo/estudante), [Professor](https://facom.ufu.br/publico-alvo/professor), [Técnico administrativo](https://facom.ufu.br/publico-alvo/tecnico-administrativo), [Comunidade externa](https://facom.ufu.br/publico-alvo/comunidade-externa)

**Data de publicação:** 24/01/2022

**Visiting Professor in Computer Science – Federal University of Uberlandia (UFU)**

**Position details**

**Employer:** Federal University of Uberlandia (UFU)

**Department:** Computer Science (FACOM)

**Knowledge area:** Computer Science Research

**Employment Type:** Full time (40 hours a week)

**Contract duration:** 12-month contract, extendable to 24 months

**Required degree:** PhD in Computer Science or Engineering

**Desired candidate profile:**

Researcher with an innovative profile and outstanding academic-scientific experience in Computer Science.

**Job Duties:**

Teach and advise in the graduate program of computer science.

Pursue research agendas in collaboration with other program's professors.

Scholarly activities related to the graduate program, including chairing and serving on student master and doctoral projects committees.

**Requirements:**

Hold a Ph.D. degree in Computer Science or Engineering, for at least five years.

Hold a position of Professor or Researcher in computer science, outside Brazil for the past 60 months.

Evidence of high-quality research and scholarly activities. A minimal requirement is at least four papers in the last five years classified in quartiles Q1 or Q2 at Web of Science, SJR, or Scopus.

**Registration Instructions:**

1.  Fill the application form and send documents at [https://forms.office.com/r/0TzqkFh6br](https://forms.office.com/r/0TzqkFh6br) (**deadline:** February 17, 2022 2:59pm GMT-03)
2.  Pay the registration fee (R$ 190.00, via Paypal Invoice or Brazilian GRU system)
3.  Send copies to [facom@ufu.br](mailto:facom@ufu.br) of the following:
    1.  ID with photo (Passport)
    2.  Proof of payment for the registration fee
    3.  Full Curriculum Vitae
    4.  Doctoral degree certificate

**Selection process criteria:**

Curriculum vitae assessment and research plan assessment. The research plan submission will be open on March 08 and 09, 2022.

Research plan presentation and interview online via Microsoft Teams (scheduled to March 14, 2022).

**Monthly salary (according to candidate's experience):**

Adjunct level - R$ 11,800.12

Associate level - R$ 16,591.91

Full Professor level - R$ 20,530.01
